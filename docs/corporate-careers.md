## Data contract
source=corporate for UI and monthly source counts; sourceKey=corporate:<companyId> for run health and observation identity. Each company has independent success, fallback, failed or skipped; validated zero is successful. Failed companies retain only their own inventory, explicitly marked unverified. ATS publication date or null, never first seen as publication date. Exact city allowlist; no generic Greece remote. Parent categories unchanged; role subcategories are additive. Monthly snapshots include coverage, company and role counts when available. Older snapshots are not comparable at constant coverage; don't reinterpret their absence as zero demand.
Current dedup prioritizes existing job boards on exact title/company matches; distinct ATS IDs otherwise remain. Fuzzy employer aliases, multiple-location variants and an independent per-company retention TTL require further work.

Lifecycle history records verified ATS observations even when a matching board listing wins public deduplication. ATS posting IDs, not title-derived URL slugs, define stable history identity. Before enabling verify each generated candidate URL resolves to the correct vacancy, and confirm API use terms; the smoke command does not publish data.

## Age policy — 2026-11-24
Frontend uses at most 30 inclusive Athens calendar days (today + 29 days), including all source types. Original source publication date (`date`) wins; unknown publication date uses durable `firstSeenAt`, labeled as discovery, never as publication. No age reference means hidden. Historical and previous inventory timestamps prevent resets on reappearance. Retain all observed corporate open postings independently of age; record observations without time-based deletion. Snapshot ageWindow separates 30-day visibility, dated publications, first-seen fallback, older, unknown, and retained totals; no backfill of historic snapshots. Notifications use the same age filter. Preview contains one deliberately old fictional job and one unknown-publication demo; no live collection before documented authorization.

The 30-day counter in snapshots describes the retained aggregator dataset (not separate direct exclusive postings), and does not pretend historical snapshots had age measurements. Future publication timestamps are excluded even if discovery timestamps are known. Manual queue records use earliest evidence available from existing inventory, observation history or verified approval; an old manual-only record not retained or observed historically cannot be retrospectively dated with certainty.
